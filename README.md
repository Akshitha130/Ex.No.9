# EX.NO.9 – EXPLORING PROMPTING TECHNIQUES FOR AI IMAGE AND VIDEO GENERATION

## AIM

To explore different prompting techniques for generating realistic AI images and videos using progressive prompt refinement.

## OBJECTIVE

* To understand AI image and video generation.
* To study basic, refined, and advanced prompts.
* To observe how prompt details affect the generated output.
* To create a realistic tiger scene using AI generation tools.

## TOOLS / AI MODELS USED

* OpenAI Sora
* Gemini AI
* Canva AI
* ChatGPT – Prompt design and refinement

## PROCEDURE

1. Select a realistic wildlife scene.
2. Create a simple prompt.
3. Generate the initial image.
4. Add details to improve the prompt.
5. Generate a refined image.
6. Create an advanced prompt with realistic environmental details.
7. Generate the final image.
8. Apply similar prompts for video generation.
9. Compare the outputs obtained from different prompt levels.

### IMAGE GENERATION





### Scenario 

A futuristic classroom is designed using Generative AI. The classroom contains smart interactive displays, holographic learning systems, AI-powered teaching assistants, virtual reality devices, robotic equipment, and modern digital learning facilities. Students interact with advanced technologies while learning in an intelligent and collaborative environment.

---





### 1. Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**

> Create a futuristic classroom where students are learning with advanced technology.

**Expected Output:**
A simple futuristic classroom containing students, modern digital devices, smart screens, and a high-tech learning environment.

**Motion Characteristics:**

* Students move naturally while learning.
* Digital screens display changing information.
* Students interact with digital devices.
* The camera remains mostly static or has minimal movement.

## GENERATION PARAMETERS TO CONSIDER




* **Resolution:** High
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
* **Lighting:** Early morning / golden light
* **Camera:** Smooth tracking shot
* **Style:** Photorealistic
* **Environment:** Dense tropical forest
* **Motion:** Natural tiger walking
* **Duration:** 5–10 seconds for video
* **Quality:** High detail




## OBSERVATION



The experiment shows that the quality and realism of AI-generated videos improve as the prompt becomes more detailed and specific. 


The basic prompt generated a simple scene of a tiger walking through a forest, but it provided limited information about the environment, movement, lighting, and camera behavior. As a result, the output was relatively simple.

The detailed prompt added important elements such as a dense forest, early-morning sunlight, light mist, and smooth camera tracking. These additional instructions resulted in better environmental details and more natural-looking movement. The tiger and its surroundings became more visually descriptive compared with the basic prompt.

The final advanced prompt provided much greater control over the generated video. It specified the tiger's body movements, tail and ear movement, camera angle, camera tracking, forest vegetation, sunlight, shadows, mist, wind movement, depth of field, and overall cinematic appearance. This resulted in a more photorealistic and wildlife-documentary-style video.

Overall, the experiment demonstrates that clear, descriptive, and well-structured prompts provide greater control over AI-generated images and videos. Gradually refining the prompt from basic to detailed and finally to an advanced version improves the realism, environmental details, motion, camera behavior, and consistency of the generated output.

## RESULT



The experiment on Exploring Prompting Techniques for AI Image and Video Generation was successfully completed. Different levels of prompts—basic, detailed, and advanced—were used to generate a realistic Bengal tiger wildlife video. The results showed that adding specific details about the subject, environment, lighting, camera movement, and motion characteristics improved the quality and realism of the generated video. The final advanced prompt produced a more cinematic, natural, and consistent wildlife scene with realistic tiger movements and environmental effects.




## Conclusion



The experiment successfully demonstrated the importance of effective prompting in AI image and video generation. By gradually improving the prompt from a basic description to a detailed and advanced prompt, better control was achieved over the subject, environment, lighting, camera movement, and motion. The final prompt produced a more realistic and cinematic wildlife video with natural tiger movements and environmental effects. Thus, well-structured and descriptive prompts can significantly improve the quality, realism, and consistency of AI-generated visual content.
